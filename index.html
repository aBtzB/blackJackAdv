New to blackjack? Here are the rules.
  • Both players start with 500 pretend chips and take turns against the dealer. Bet any whole number from 1 up to your available chips. Player 1 goes first.
  • Hit takes another card. Stand ends your turn.
  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. More than 21 is a bust.
  • Number cards use their number. J, Q and K are 10. An ace is 11 or 1, whichever helps your hand.
  • An ace and a ten-value card in your first two cards is blackjack. It beats a 21 made with more cards.
  • The dealer reveals its hidden card after both turns, then draws until reaching at least 17. It stands on soft 17 too.
  • The dealer checks for blackjack immediately. Blackjack or a total of 21 automatically finishes your turn.
  • Your bet comes out of your chips when the cards are dealt. A regular win returns your stake plus the same amount in profit. A tie (push) returns your stake. A loss keeps it deducted.
  • Blackjack returns your stake plus 1.5 times your bet in profit, rounded down to a whole chip for odd bets. A bet of 5 earns 7 chips in profit.
  • The result shows your gain or loss for that hand. For a bet of 50, a regular win shows +50 and blackjack shows +75. These are pretend amounts.
  • Every round uses a freshly shuffled 52-card deck. Winning adds one to your win count. Both players can win the same round.
  • If both players bust or have blackjack, extra dealer cards cannot change their results, so the dealer just reveals its hand.

If a player has 0 chips after a round, they lose and the table ends. If both run out together, both lose. Start a new table to play again. Refreshing also resets chips and wins. This is a local game for two people sharing one screen, using no real money.
